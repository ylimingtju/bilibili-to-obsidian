# 学习目标 / Learning goals

我希望能用视频中的方法独立判断经济变化、公司经营和资金配置。可以把这句话改成自己的学习目标。

My starting goal is to use the methods in a video to reason about the economy, companies and investment choices. Replace this goal with your own.

每期先判断视频类型，再展开 1～3 个能在下一次判断中用到的要点。只使用讲出来的内容，保留字幕链接和分 P 时间戳。首次出现的术语用一句话解释，区分作者原意与助手推断。

Identify the type of video. Choose one to three ideas worth reusing, cite their transcript and timestamps, and explain new terms in one sentence. Use only the spoken content and attribute inferences to the assistant.
